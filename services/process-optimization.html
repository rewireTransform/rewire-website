// Process Optimization

Process optimization for small and mid-sized businesses

Work moves slowly because nobody has seen the whole path. We map how work actually gets done, cut the steps that don't earn their place, and stay until the new process is running.

Start with a Business DiagnosticHow it works →

Signs you need this

  • Work waits on one person, or on an email nobody owns.
  • The same data gets typed into two or three systems.
  • Hand-offs between sales, operations, and finance drop things.
  • Nobody can say how long an order, a job, or a month-end close really takes.
  • Growing means hiring more people to repeat the same manual steps.

What we do

Map the real process

We walk the work with the people who do it, not the people who manage it. You get one page that shows every step, hand-off, and wait.

Find the drag

We look for rework, approvals that add nothing, duplicate entry, and queues that build up. Then we rank them by what they cost you.

Redesign it

We design the simpler version, with clear owners and a short list of measures that show whether it works.

Write it down and train the team

SOPs, checklists, and hands-on training, so the new process survives the first busy week.

How an engagement runs

  1. DiagnoseWe map the current process and measure where time and effort go. This is the Business Diagnostic.
  2. DesignWe design the improved process and agree a fixed scope with you before anything changes.
  3. DeployWe work alongside your team to roll it out, adjust it in real use, and train the people who run it.
  4. OptimizeWe check the measures, fix what slipped, and report results. Monthly support is available if you want ongoing help.

Questions we hear

What does a process optimization engagement look like?

It starts with a Business Diagnostic: we map the current process, find where time and effort are lost, and recommend what to fix first. If you proceed, we design the new process, roll it out with your team, and report results.

Do we need new software to fix our processes?

Often not. We fix the process first, because automating a broken process only makes it fail faster. If a tool is the right answer, we tell you which one and why.

How do you charge for process work?

We scope projects at a fixed price after the Business Diagnostic, so you know the cost before work starts. Monthly retainers are available for ongoing optimization.
