// Business Intelligence

Business intelligence and dashboards for small businesses

You already have the data. It sits in QuickBooks, your CRM, and a dozen spreadsheets that disagree with each other. We connect it, clean it, and put the numbers you run the business on in front of you.

Start with a Business DiagnosticHow it works →

Signs you need this

  • Someone rebuilds the monthly report by hand, and it is out of date when it lands.
  • Two spreadsheets give two different answers to the same question.
  • When you ask a simple question about margin, pipeline, or cash, the answer takes days.
  • Your data lives in accounting, a CRM, and operations tools that never talk to each other.
  • Big decisions get made on gut feel because the numbers are too slow to pull.

What we do

Agree on the questions

We start with the decisions you make each week and the handful of KPIs behind them. We skip the 40-chart dashboard nobody opens.

Connect the sources

We pull from QuickBooks, Xero, your CRM, spreadsheets, and operational systems into one consistent set of numbers.

Build dashboards that get used

We build in Power BI, Looker Studio, or Google Sheets, whichever fits your team and budget, and set them to refresh without anyone touching them.

Hand it over

We document every definition and train your team to read, question, and extend the dashboards.

How an engagement runs

  1. DiagnoseWe list the decisions you need to make, find where the data lives, and check how trustworthy it is. This is the Business Diagnostic.
  2. DesignWe design the KPI set and the dashboard layout, and agree a fixed scope with you.
  3. DeployWe connect the data, build and test the dashboards with your team, and fix definitions that don't match reality.
  4. OptimizeWe watch how the dashboards get used, tune them, and add new views as questions change.

Questions we hear

Which BI tools do you use?

We work in Power BI, Looker Studio, and Google Sheets, and connect to QuickBooks, Xero, and common CRMs. We choose the tool that fits your team and budget, not the other way around.

Do we need a data warehouse?

Most small businesses don't need one at the start. We connect your systems directly and add a warehouse only when data volume or complexity calls for it.

Can you work with the reports we already have?

Yes. We start from the reports your team already relies on, find where the numbers disagree, and build from there.
