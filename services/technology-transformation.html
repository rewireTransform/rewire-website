// Technology Transformation

Technology consulting for small and mid-sized businesses

Most small businesses don't have a technology problem. They have too many tools, bought one at a time, that don't fit how the work runs. We audit what you have, decide what stays, and manage the change.

Start with a Business DiagnosticHow it works →

Signs you need this

  • You pay for software that few people use.
  • Your systems don't share data, so people bridge the gaps by hand.
  • Tools get picked off a demo, without checking how they fit your process.
  • You want to use AI but don't know where it would pay off.
  • A past rollout stalled because the team never adopted it.

What we do

Audit the stack

We inventory every tool, what it costs, who uses it, and where tools overlap or leave gaps.

Match tools to the work

We start from how your processes run, then recommend what to keep, replace, connect, or cut.

Select and plan

When you need something new, we set the criteria with you, compare the real options, and plan the rollout and data migration.

Manage the change

We train your people, handle the awkward first weeks, and track adoption, because a tool nobody uses has no return.

How an engagement runs

  1. DiagnoseWe audit your tools, costs, and workflows to see what earns its place. This is the Business Diagnostic.
  2. DesignWe design the target stack and the rollout plan, and agree a fixed scope with you.
  3. DeployWe work alongside your team through setup, migration, and training.
  4. OptimizeWe measure adoption and results, resolve issues, and adjust the plan.

Questions we hear

What does a technology audit cover?

We inventory your tools, costs, and users, map them against how your work runs, and recommend what to keep, replace, connect, or cut. You get a prioritized plan, not a long report.

Can you help us decide where to use AI?

Yes. We look for specific tasks where AI saves real time, such as sorting, drafting, and data extraction, and test them on your own data before you commit.

Will you manage the rollout, or only advise?

We stay through the rollout. Our work isn't done when we hand you a document. It's done when the new setup is running and your team is using it.
