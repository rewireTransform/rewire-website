// Automation Engineering

Workflow automation for small and mid-sized businesses

If your team copies data between systems, chases approvals by email, or assembles reports by hand, that time can come back. We build the automation, connect your tools, and stay until it runs without us.

Start with a Business DiagnosticHow it works →

Signs you need this

  • People copy and paste between your CRM, accounting system, and spreadsheets.
  • New leads sit for hours or days before anyone follows up.
  • Invoices and approvals move through email chains.
  • Month-end reports take a person a full day or more to assemble.
  • Errors keep showing up from manual re-keying.

What we do

Pick what is worth automating

We rank tasks by hours spent, error rate, and how often they repeat. The best candidates come first.

Build it with the right tool

We use Make.com, n8n, Python, and direct API integrations. Where a step needs judgment, such as sorting email or pulling fields from a document, we add an AI step with the OpenAI API and a human check.

Make it dependable

Every automation gets error handling, alerts, and logs, so a failure shows up in your inbox, not in a customer complaint.

Document and transfer

You get a plain-language record of what each automation does, what it touches, and who owns it.

How an engagement runs

  1. DiagnoseWe list your manual tasks, time them, and rank them by payoff. This is the Business Diagnostic.
  2. DesignWe design each automation, the systems it touches, and the checks around it, and agree a fixed scope with you.
  3. DeployWe build, test with real data, and switch automations on alongside your team, with a fallback if something fails.
  4. OptimizeWe monitor results, fix issues, and add new automations as you find more work to hand off.

Questions we hear

What kinds of work can be automated?

Repetitive, rule-based work: moving data between systems, lead routing and follow-up, invoice and approval flows, report assembly, and document intake. We tell you early if a task isn't worth automating.

Will automation replace my staff?

The goal is to remove the repetitive steps so your people spend their time on exceptions, customers, and decisions. We design automations with a person in the loop wherever judgment matters.

What happens if an automation breaks?

We build in error handling, alerts, and logs. You know when something fails and why, and the documentation tells your team how to respond. Ongoing support is available if you want us to monitor it.
